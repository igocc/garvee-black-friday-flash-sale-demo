What changes in a real Shopify build

This demo
  • Browser Cookie limits one casual vote
  • Buttons simulate sign-in, booking, and share
  • Inventory and five-minute hold are visual states
  • Demo controls preview every campaign phase
Real Shopify
  • App Proxy + database owns votes and eligibility
  • Scheduler opens one slot every two hours
  • Shopify inventory arbitrates the final two units
  • Checkout Validation enforces eligibility where available

A normal Shopify cart does not reserve inventory for five minutes. The hold shown here is a product-flow concept that needs a separate production design.
